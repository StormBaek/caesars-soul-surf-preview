Awareness & education

Surfers for Science

People who spend their lives by the ocean have a close connection to its changes. Surfers for Science brings that perspective to conversations about nature, science and the future of our planet.

Richard uses the platform to share environmental ideas and encourage people to care for the places that bring us together.

Close to home

Small actions.
Real care.

Along the shore

Collecting litter on beach visits and helping marine animals and seabirds in distress are part of Richard’s care for the coast.

In the community

That care reaches beyond the water, too — including looking after a colony of stray cats with food, shelter and affection.

A graphic novel with a purpose

The Balance

What if there were surfers in outer space? That question grew into Richard’s science-fiction graphic novel project, bringing together cosmic imagination and concern for our planet.

Through stories of other worlds, The Balance: Stories of a Type II Civilization explores environmental destruction and invites readers to think about the future of our own.
