Our mission — the adventure

Surfing connects us to the world around us. We explore Peru’s coast, share our love of the ocean and help people build confidence in the waves.

The company

Meet Richard

Richard Cedano has surfed and taught in Santa Cruz, California, the Canary Islands and his native Peru. His approach combines practical coaching with a personal connection to the ocean and the surfing community.

Origins

How it all started

The story began in California, where Richard spent a decade exploring the waves with his faithful dog, Caesar. Back in Peru, they travelled together in a classic 1964 Kombi T1. After Caesar passed away, Richard continued their journey — sharing surfing and adventure with people from around the world, in Caesar’s memory.

Sustainability

Preserve nature

For Richard, caring for the ocean is part of being a surfer. Through Surfers for Science, beach cleanups and care for local wildlife, that commitment reaches beyond the waves.
