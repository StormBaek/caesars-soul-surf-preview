Surf adventure in Peru - In no way ordinary

Surfing in Peru is an experience like no other – the stunning coast offers waves from beginners to experts, creating a truly unforgettable adventure. Join our surf trip and take lessons with our expert instructors to discover the thrill of catching waves in one of the world’s most beautiful surfing destinations.

Surf trip experience

Discover Peru’s wild coast

Join us on an unforgettable surf trip in our camper van along the unique coastline of Peru. With over 300 surf spots to explore, including hidden beaches and secret surf spots only accessible by van, you’ll catch some of the world’s best waves while experiencing breathtaking scenery and vibrant local culture. With the surfboards strapped to the roof, let’s hit the road and make memories that will last forever!

Surfing lessons Lima

From Beginner to Advanced

Ready to learn to surf or improve your skills? Our personalized surf lessons around Lima will take your surfing to the next level. With free choice of surf spot depending on the conditions and state-of-the-art video analysis technology, our experienced instructors will tailor each lesson to your individual needs. From beginners to advanced surfers, we’ve got you covered. Join us for a fun and memorable experience in the water and let us help you catch some waves!

About us

25 Years of Surfing Experience

We have been passionate about surfing for over 25 years, exploring the world’s most beautiful surf spots and sharing our knowledge and expertise with others. Our mission is to provide a unique and unforgettable surfing experience that goes beyond just catching waves. We believe that surfing is not just a sport, but a way of life that brings people together and creates lasting connections. That’s why our focus is on personalized instruction, creating a fun and friendly atmosphere, and fostering a sense of community both in and out of the water.
