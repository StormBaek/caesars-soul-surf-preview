Do I need any surfing experience?

Not at all. Private and group lessons welcome beginners, with coaching adapted to your level. If you already surf, advanced coaching gives you space to work on your technique.

Should I choose a lesson or a surf trip?

A lesson is a simple way to get in the water around Lima. For more time exploring, choose a full day on the coast, a two-day surf and camp experience, or a longer journey through Peru. Richard can help you choose around your dates and experience.

Do I need to bring my own board or wetsuit?

Surfboards and wetsuits are included in the lesson packages. Surf equipment is also included in the trips. If you prefer to bring your own equipment, let Richard know when you get in touch.

Where do we meet?

Lessons take place around Lima, with the surf spot chosen for the conditions. Lesson packages include transport to the surf spot, and pickup from your accommodation is included in several trip packages. Confirm the meeting point and time with Richard when arranging your day.

Can children join a lesson?

Tandem lessons give younger children a first experience of surfing on a board shared with an instructor. Tell Richard your child’s age and confidence in the water so he can help you choose a suitable lesson.

What is included in a surf trip?

It depends on the trip. The day trip includes two surf sessions, pickup, breakfast and surf equipment. The two-day camp adds an overnight camping experience, while longer trips include private room accommodation. You can compare the full inclusions on the surf trips page.

How do I book?

Send Richard a message on WhatsApp with your preferred dates, number of people and surfing experience. He’ll help you choose your lesson or trip and confirm the current price, availability and arrangements with you.
